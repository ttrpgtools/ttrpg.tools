A character sheet manager for [Blackfisk Publishing's](https://blackfiskpublishing.com/) [Heroes of Cerulea RPG](https://blackfiskpublishing.com/products/heroes-of-cerulea/).

<Image src={screenshot} alt="Screenshot of the Cerulean Hero interface" />

Hero sheets with basic roll automation and inventory management. Sheets are saved locally on your device, but you can [sign up](https://cerulean.ttrpg.tools/auth/signup) for a free TTRPG.tools account to allow for saving your characters online. With an account you also get access to create campaigns to link players together for visibility and loot distribution.
