Starter material comes in several forms. A **complete game** contains everything needed for continued play. A **quickstart** or **test drive** usually provides enough rules, pre-generated characters and an adventure for a session or two. **Basic rules**, previews and playtests may require the game master to supply more of the adventure or supporting material.

The badges beside each link summarize what is available. “GM-ready” means the download includes enough material to run an introductory session, not that it contains the full game.

## Complete Games (free)

- [Ironsworn](https://tomkinpress.com/products/ironsworn-digital-edition) - The complete dark-fantasy game, including a rulebook, play kit and printable asset cards. Supports guided play with a game master, cooperative play and solo play.

## Quickstarts & Test Drives (free)

- [Shadowdark](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/products/shadowdark-rpg-quickstart-set-pdf) - Player and game master guides, eight pre-made characters and an introductory adventure for levels 1–3.
- [MUTANT: Year Zero](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/188014/mutant-year-zero-starter-booklet) - Basic rules, pre-generated characters and a post-apocalyptic introductory adventure.
- [ICRPG](https://icrpgcommunitycontent.com/product/index-card-rpg-free-quickstart/) - The Index Card RPG quickstart rules and material for getting a game running quickly.
- [Savage Worlds](https://peginc.com/product/deadlands-the-weird-west-blood-on-the-range-savage-worlds-test-drive-swade) - A Weird West adventure that introduces the genre-neutral Savage Worlds rules.
- [Fabula Ultima](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/411240/fabula-ultima-ttjrpg-press-start) - Basic rules, pre-generated characters and an introductory JRPG-inspired adventure.
- [MÖRK BORG](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1GcysVYxEklrDCva3gSkYqXvS2vy3Kbdu/view?usp=sharing) - The free Bare Bones rules preview for the doom-metal dark-fantasy game. [Direct Google Drive link]
- [Call of Cthulhu](https://www.chaosium.com/cthulhu-quickstart/) - Quickstart rules and the classic introductory adventure *The Haunting*.
- [Daggerheart](https://www.daggerheart.com/getting-started/) - The Sablewood Messengers adventure, five pre-generated characters and mechanical overviews. The same page links to the free system reference document for the complete rules.
- [Dragonbane](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/shop/dragonbane/free-quickstart-pdf/) - The complete introductory adventure *Riddermound* and five pre-generated characters.
- [Cyberpunk RED: Easy Mode](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/409912/cyberpunk-red-easy-mode) - Introductory rules, five pre-generated characters, maps and the mission *Getting Paid*.

## Basic Rules, Previews & Playtests (free)

- [Nimble](https://nimblerpg.com/pages/start) - Quickstart basic rules, pre-made heroes and short how-to-play videos.
- [Dungeons & Dragons](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024) - The 2024 Basic Rules. D&D Beyond also provides a character builder and introductory guidance.
- [DC20](https://thedungeoncoach.com/pages/dc20-free-downloads) - Earlier playtest rules and a one-shot adventure. The current beta rules are a separate paid product.

## Starter Box Sets (paid)

- [D&D Heroes of the Borderlands](https://marketplace.dndbeyond.com/category/4705000) - A board-game-style introduction to D&D, available in physical and digital editions.
- [Pathfinder Beginner Box](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-beginner-box-secrets-of-the-unlit-star/) - Rules for players and game masters, a ready-to-run adventure, pre-made characters, dice and cardboard pawns.
