There are TTRPG systems for nearly every kind of story. Some are free and compact, while others span shelves of core books, settings and adventures. I have only played a portion of the games below, so the descriptions are intended as short signposts rather than comprehensive reviews.

Genre is flexible. A single game may support several tones or styles, but each one is listed where its most recognizable experience provides a useful starting point. Some systems also have [[Starter Kits]] that let you try them before buying a core book.

## Fantasy

### Epic & Heroic Fantasy

- [Dungeons & Dragons (5e, 2014/2024)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/) — the mainstream fantasy juggernaut with a vast adventure ecosystem.
- [Pathfinder (2e)](https://paizo.com/pathfinder) — crunch-forward fantasy with deep character options and robust adventure paths.
- [Daggerheart](https://www.daggerheart.com/) — collaborative heroic fantasy driven by Hope and Fear, character relationships and shared worldbuilding.
- [Nimble](https://nimblerpg.com) — fast, tactical 5e-compatible fantasy tuned to minimize slog and spotlight choices.
- [Legend in the Mist](https://sonofoak.com/en-gb/pages/legend-in-the-mist) — rustic fantasy about folk heroes in a narrative-focused adventure.
- [Index Card RPG (ICRPG)](https://www.runehammer.online/index-card-rpg) — DIY-friendly d20 toolkit that prizes speed and table flow.
- [DC20](https://thedungeoncoach.com/pages/dc20) — crunchy-but-approachable fantasy from The Dungeon Coach.
- [Draw Steel](https://shop.mcdmproductions.com/collections/draw-steel) — action-oriented fantasy RPG by game designer and YouTuber Matt Colville.
- [Tales of the Valiant](https://www.talesofthevaliant.com) — 5e-compatible rebuild with new classes, monsters and rules options.
- [Dragonbane](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/dragonbane/) — classic Swedish fantasy revived as fast, dangerous and adventurous play.
- [The One Ring (2e)](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/the-one-ring/) — lore-first licensed Middle-earth role-playing.
- [Enclave](https://vortakgames.com/products/enclave-rpg-advent-edition-by-robby-howell) — diceless, narrative-first fantasy about teamwork.

### Cozy, Whimsical & Fairy-Tale Fantasy

- [Mausritter](https://mausritter.com/) — rules-light adventures about brave mice in a huge and dangerous world, blending whimsy with old-school peril.
- [Keeper's Call](https://jpcoovert.com/products/keepers-call) — rules-light d6 fantasy with fast character creation, concise rules and a hand-drawn style.
- [Wanderhome](https://possumcreekgames.com/pages/wanderhome) — pastoral fantasy about travelling animal-folk, changing seasons and life after conflict.
- [Dolmenwood](https://necroticgnome.com/pages/about-dolmenwood) — old-school adventure in an eerie fairy-tale forest inspired by British folklore.
- [Brighter Worlds](https://www.brighterworldsrpg.com/) — whimsical fantasy adventures built from Cairn.

### JRPG-Inspired Fantasy

- [Fabula Ultima](https://need.games/fabula-ultima) — cinematic fantasy about bonds, character styles and over-the-top arcs.
- [BREAK!!](https://breakrpg.com/) — anime- and SNES-flavoured fantasy emphasizing teamwork, clarity and accessibility.
- [Legends of Akeroth](https://crossedpaths.itch.io/legends-of-akeroth) — hopeful, nostalgic JRPG fantasy supporting guided, group, duet and solo play.

### Old-School, Dungeons & Exploration

- [Cairn](https://cairnrpg.com/) — rules-light fantasy about dangerous forests, inventory play and emergent peril.
- [Knave (2e)](https://questingbeast.itch.io/knave-second-edition) — lightweight, slot-based OSR built for exploration and fast preparation.
- [Maze Rats](https://questingbeast.itch.io/maze-rats) — ultra-compact OSR with large random tables and speedy character generation.
- [Old-School Essentials (OSE)](https://necroticgnome.com/pages/about-old-school-essentials) — faithful B/X retroclone with clear rules and layout.
- [Dungeon Crawl Classics (DCC)](https://goodman-games.com/dungeon-crawl-classics-rpg/) — gonzo Appendix-N fantasy with zero-level funnels and wild magic.
- [Forbidden Lands](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/forbidden-lands/) — hexcrawl survival fantasy with strong exploration and base-building.
- [Shadowdark](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/pages/shadowdark) — modern old-school play with real-time torch timers and a classic dungeon ethos.
- [Crown & Skull](https://www.runehammer.online/crown-skull-rpg) — hex-driven dark-fantasy “world engine” with tactical foes.
- [Tiny Dungeon (2e)](https://gallantknightgames.com/products/tiny-dungeon-second-edition) — minimalist fantasy on the TinyD6 chassis, suited to quick starts. ([TinyD6 also covers other genres.](https://gallantknightgames.com/collections/corebooks/TinyD6))
- [The Black Hack](https://the-black-hack.jehaisleprintemps.net/) — streamlined OSR core with many hacks and community-created games.
- [Five Torches Deep](https://www.fivetorchesdeep.com/) — 5e-meets-OSR dungeon crawling with resource pressure.
- [HackMaster](https://kenzerco.com/product-category/hackmaster/) — detailed, tongue-in-cheek fantasy descended from an AD&D parody.
- [Worlds Without Number](https://sine-nomine-publishing.myshopify.com/collections/worlds-without-number) — classic fantasy adventuring paired with extensive sandbox-building tools.

### Solo & Cooperative Fantasy

- [Ironsworn](https://tomkinpress.com/pages/ironsworn) — quest-driven dark fantasy designed for solo, cooperative or game-master-guided play. The complete digital edition is free.

### Heists & Strange Cities

- [Blades in the Dark](https://bladesinthedark.com/) — industrial ghost-punk capers with crew development, position and effect, downtime and stress.

## Horror

### Cosmic & Conspiracy Horror

- [Call of Cthulhu](https://www.chaosium.com/call-of-cthulhu-rpg) — investigative cosmic horror using BRP, with sanity and relentless dread.
- [Delta Green](https://www.delta-green.com/) — modern conspiracy horror about agents confronting the Mythos, damaged bonds and burnout.

### Gothic, Folk & Urban Horror

- [Vampire: The Masquerade (V5)](https://renegadegamestudios.com/vampire) — personal and political horror among secret vampire societies.
- [Monster of the Week](https://evilhat.com/product/monster-of-the-week/) — PbtA monster hunting in the vein of *Buffy* and *Supernatural*.
- [Vaesen](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/vaesen/) — Nordic folklore investigations with a gothic tone.
- [MÖRK BORG](https://morkborg.com/) — doom-metal, art-heavy, end-times dark fantasy.

## Cyberpunk & Near-Future

- [Cities Without Number](https://sine-nomine-publishing.myshopify.com/collections/cities-without-number) — cyberpunk sandbox sibling to Stars Without Number, with faction tools and missions.
- [Cyberpunk RED](https://rtalsoriangames.com/cyberpunk/) — official continuation of the *Cyberpunk* line, set after 2020 and before 2077.
- [Technoir](https://www.technoirrpg.com) — gritty, high-tech and hard-boiled adventure.
- [Neon City Overdrive](https://perilplanet.itch.io/neon-city-overdrive) — fast-playing cyberpunk action using an intuitive d6 dice pool.
- [Zaibatsu](https://www.zozergames.com/zaibatsu.html) — *Neuromancer*-inspired dystopian action in future Tokyo.

---

## Contemporary Action & Mystery

- [Everyday Heroes](https://evilgeniusgames.com/product-line/everyday-heroes/) — modern d20 action with chases, hacking, hazards and licensed blockbuster settings.
- [Kids on Bikes](https://www.huntersentertainment.com/kidsonbikesrpg) — collaborative, rules-light adventures about small-town heroes facing big mysteries.

---

## Universal Systems & Toolkits

- [Savage Worlds (SWADE)](https://peginc.com/savage-settings/savage-worlds/) — a fast-playing multi-genre engine with many published settings.
- [GURPS](https://www.sjgames.com/gurps/) — detailed universal toolkit for constructing and playing in nearly any genre.
- [Cypher System](https://cypher-system.com/) — narrative-forward universal engine in which players make most of the rolls.
- [Fate](https://evilhat.com/product/fate-core-system/) — flexible, fiction-focused system built around aspects, skills and fate points.

## Science Fiction & Science Fantasy

### Hard & Exploratory Science Fiction

- [Traveller](https://www.mongoosepublishing.com/collections/core-rulebooks-traveller) — classic far-future science fiction about travellers, starships, trade and exploration.
- [Stars Without Number](https://sine-nomine-publishing.myshopify.com/collections/stars-without-number) — sector-building sandbox science fiction with OSR roots.
- [Coriolis – The Third Horizon](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/coriolis/) — Arabian-nights-in-space adventures involving mysteries, factions and icons.
- [Diaspora](https://evilhat.com/product/diaspora) — Fate-based hard science fiction about small crews and collaboratively mapped worlds.

### Survival Stories

- [Mothership](https://www.tuesdayknightgames.com/pages/mothership-rpg) — survival-horror science fiction with panic and stress at the forefront.
- [ALIEN: The Roleplaying Game](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/alien/) — official *Alien* survival horror using the Year Zero Engine.

### Mecha & Tactical Science Fiction

- [Lancer](https://massifpress.com/lancer) — far-future pilots and highly customizable mechs, pairing narrative play with detailed tactical combat.

### Weird Science Fantasy

- [Into the Odd (Remastered)](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/into-the-odd/) — brutal, treasure-driven exploration with elegant minimal rules.
- [Tales from the Loop](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/tales-from-the-loop/) — kids solving mysteries in a retro-futurist 1980s that never was.
- [Troika!](https://www.troikarpg.com/) — planar-hopping science fantasy with exuberant weirdness.
- [Vaults of Vaarn](https://vaultsofvaarn.com/) — dying-earth, blue-desert science-fantasy zine RPG.

## Post-Apocalyptic & Military Survival

- [Mutant: Year Zero](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/mutant-year-zero/) — post-apocalyptic survival and community-building in the Zone.
- [Twilight: 2000 (4e)](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/twilight-2000/) — sandbox survival after World War III in an alternate 2000.
- [Fallout: The Roleplaying Game (2d20)](https://modiphius.net/pages/fallout-the-roleplaying-game) — official role-playing in Fallout's retro-futurist wasteland.
